## What this source does

- Foreground tap-to-talk, encrypted local pairing state, authenticated HTTPS bridge health/upload and reply playback; unknown upload admission is not blindly retried.
- Opt-in **Hey Sarah** offline keyword listener with Android foreground-service notification and explicit Stop; listener, capture, and playback ownership gates guard handoffs. Manual tap remains fallback. Assistant-role gesture opens the app; it does not itself grant recording permission.
- Code21 corrected an offscreen cleanup owner-reference error (`this@OffscreenCommand`) after native ACK. Code22 uses PCM-energy speech presence and four continuous seconds without voiced PCM for tap/unlocked wake command endpointing. No speech or 30-second cap discards. The cue starts after native recording reports ready and drains its short acoustic tail before retaining command PCM. Actual beep audibility and real-speech endpoint timing remain unproven on phone.

Microphone requires opt-in/permission. Do not put provider or home secrets on the device; use a revocable bridge token. Local reset does not erase server history, and OS cache may retain temporary reply audio after process death. No live bridge endpoint, token, private CA, signer or personal transcript is published.

## Build source and limits

JDK 17, Android SDK 35 and Gradle 8.11.1 wrapper. The **compiled upstream sherpa-onnx AAR is intentionally not in Git**. Retrieve the official v2.0.1 artifact from the URL in [KWS-DEPENDENCIES.md](KWS-DEPENDENCIES.md) as `app/libs/sherpa-onnx-2.0.1.aar` and verify SHA-256 `a8d09d1d32f3eb8520482c6d3d43fae674681c5b050385967c70a3c2c9bf0ee7` before building; Gradle's `verifySherpaAar` task also rejects a mismatch. Model inference assets and required notice texts are included under `app/src/main/assets/`. SDKs, downloaded dependencies and caches are not included.

`app/build.gradle.kts` retains its local review signing configuration, which reads `.local-review/review.jks` and `.local-review/signing-pass`. Those files are private and absent. A separately approved local signer is required for the configured signed debug build; do not regenerate or publish the phone-approved signer. Do not treat a locally rebuilt APK as identical to the published code22 APK. Android instrumentation/device-fixture tests are omitted from this public current snapshot because their audio fixtures and test-server PKCS#12 private key are excluded. Historical `ScreenshotPolicyTest.kt` remains from code3; published unit tests under `app/src/test/` are source only. The code22 development report records 143 passing unit tests and compilation of Android instrumentation source; no tests were rerun for this checkpoint. Build and test histories refer to the separate local development workspace, not this checkout.
